# PYPY Final UI/UX Visual Polish Changelog

## Visual system

- Replaced the remaining dark sidebar treatment with a bright white/cyan branded navigation surface.
- Added page-specific gradient icon identities, dotted energy-grid hero patterns, soft geometric accents, stronger typography, and larger readable status values.
- Refined semantic colour use across grid, detection, AI, recovery, simulation, health, reports, and settings without changing any data logic.
- Increased card depth, radius, spacing, icon scale, hover feedback, and touch sizing while preserving reduced-motion behaviour.

## Page polish

- Overview: upgraded semantic KPI cards, Digital Twin framing, legends, threat gauge, recommendation treatment, and model/incident hierarchy.
- Live Grid: strengthened the topology hero treatment and live-network framing without changing topology semantics.
- Cyber Detection: added a coral/orange security identity and stronger threat/affected-asset hierarchy.
- AI Decision: added violet/blue pipeline identity and differentiated evidence, validation, and recovery stages.
- Self-Healing: strengthened green/cyan recovery identity and the Before → Proposed → Restored narrative.
- Attack Simulation: refined the distinct light simulation banner and interactive controls.
- Logs & Forensics: improved table headers, row density, hover state, and filters.
- System Health: upgraded service signals into friendly, responsive status cards.
- Reports: added three live-evidence report cards and improved the situation-summary hero.
- Settings: improved grouping, spacing, control hierarchy, and explanatory treatment.

## Scope

- Routes and hash routing were not changed.
- Backend, API, MQTT, WebSocket, Digital Twin, AI, FLISR, sandbox, and orchestrator contracts were not changed.
- No metrics or service states were fabricated.
- No dependency was added.
