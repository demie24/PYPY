# PYPY Grid — Commercial Readiness Report This document certifies the commercial launch readiness of the PYPY Grid Smart-Grid Cybersecurity SaaS Platform, detailing the monetization structure, payment integrations, legal compliance, and customer onboarding wizard. --- ## 1. Product Monetization Strategy PYPY Grid is positioned as a specialized B2B and Academic SaaS platform targeting research universities, power distribution utilities, and cybersecurity consultancies. The product operates on a tiered monthly/yearly subscription model, structured as follows: ### Free Tier (Trial) - **Price**: RM 0 - **Target**: Individual students and independent researchers. - **Limits**: Access to IEEE 14-bus topology only. Maximum 5 simulation runs per month. Community support via GitHub issues. Local storage limited to 100 MB. ### Academic Premium Tier - **Price**: RM 19 / month (or RM 190 / year) - **Target**: University graduate students, professors, and academic labs. - **Features**: Unlocks all standard IEEE topologies (IEEE 14, 39, 57, 118-bus). Unlimited simulation runs. Access to BCM Center (islanding calculations and vulnerability index). Email support within 48 hours. AI Copilot research assistant limited to 500 prompts per month. ### Research Lab Tier - **Price**: RM 49 / month (or RM 490 / year) - **Target**: Professional utility researchers and cybersecurity teams. - **Features**: Everything in Academic Premium. Unlimited AI Copilot prompts. Team collaboration workspaces (up to 5 seats). Access to API endpoints for automated test sweeps. Custom scenario creator. Priority email support within 12 hours. ### Enterprise Tier - **Price**: Custom Pricing (Billed annually) - **Target**: Power utilities, grid operators, national laboratories. - **Features**: On-premise deployment options. Dedicated compute instances. Custom SCADA protocol mappings. Unlimited seats. SLA-backed priority support. Dedicated account manager. --- ## 2. Integrated Payment Providers To support seamless subscription upgrades, the platform integrates two production billing adapters, maintaining provider abstraction: ### Stripe Integration (International markets) The Stripe adapter manages checkout sessions, recurring billing cycles, and invoice distribution. Webhook handlers listen for `checkout.session.completed` and `customer.subscription.deleted` events to dynamically upgrade or revert tenant plan tiers in the database. - **HMAC Security**: All incoming webhooks are validated using Stripe signature headers (`stripe-signature`) against `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. ### ToyyibPay Integration (Malaysian academic market) Given the primary focus on the Malaysian academic market, ToyyibPay has been integrated as the local payment gateway. The ToyyibPay adapter handles redirection checkouts and parses successful callbacks (`status=1`) securely via MD5-HMAC verification. --- ## 3. Email Infrastructure & Customer Communication To maintain high user retention, PYPY Grid utilizes a transactional email notification system with branded HTML templates. The system is driver-agnostic, supporting standard SMTP, Gmail API, SendGrid, and Mailgun. Emails triggered by the system include: 1. **Welcome Email**: Sent upon successful email verification, outlining initial setup steps and platform resources. 2. **Verify Email**: Dispatched during registration containing the secure 24-hour verification link. 3. **Reset Password**: Sent on user request with a 2-hour secure token. 4. **Subscription Activated**: Notifies user of plan upgrades upon payment clearance. 5. **Invoice Email**: Dispatches tax-compliant invoice details. 6. **Trial Expiring / Expired**: Warning sent to free/trial users prior to experiment locking. 7. **Simulation Finished**: Notifies researchers of completed long-running simulation sweeps. --- ## 4. First-Time Onboarding Wizard To reduce customer churn, new users are guided through the **Workspace Setup Wizard**: 1. **Welcome Screen**: Explains the platform. 2. **Profile Creation**: Captures title, biography, and primary research focus. 3. **Institution Details**: Registers university/organization name and country (Malaysian institutions are automatically flagged for academic discounts). 4. **Research Preferences**: Prompts user for areas of interest (e.g., FDIA, relay protection, SCADA security) to feed the AI recommendation engine. 5. **Topology Setup**: Selects their default bus topology (e.g., IEEE-39). 6. **First Run Configuration**: Prompts the user to queue their first test simulation to immediately experience the platform. --- ## 5.
