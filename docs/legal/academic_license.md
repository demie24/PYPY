## Preamble

This Academic License Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **PYPY Grid Sdn. Bhd.**, a company incorporated in Malaysia ("Licensor"), and the academic institution identified in the institutional registration or Order Form ("Institution" or "Licensee"), and governs the Institution's access to and use of the PYPY Grid Smart Grid Cybersecurity Research Platform ("Platform").

This Agreement applies to the **Academic Premium** (RM 19/month per seat) and institutional bulk licences accessed under verified academic status. Individual academic users are bound by the PYPY Grid [Terms of Service](terms_of_service.md); this Agreement supplements those Terms with provisions specific to institutional academic use.

By registering for an institutional account, the authorised representative of the Institution confirms acceptance of this Agreement on behalf of the Institution.

## 1. Grant of Licence

### 1.1 Scope of Licence
Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, PYPY Grid hereby grants to the Institution a **limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence** to access and use the Platform, including its simulation engines, grid topology models, attack scenario libraries, AI Copilot, and associated documentation, solely for the purposes described in Section 2.

### 1.2 Authorised Users
The licence covers the following authorised user categories within the Institution:
- Faculty members, researchers, and academic staff of the Institution;
- Graduate students (PhD, master's, honours) enrolled at the Institution;
- Undergraduate students enrolled in accredited cybersecurity, electrical engineering, or related programmes, when accessing the Platform as part of formal coursework;
- Research fellows and postdoctoral researchers affiliated with the Institution.

The Institution is responsible for managing and revoking access for users who leave the Institution or cease to be eligible.

### 1.3 Number of Seats
The number of concurrent authorised users is specified in the institutional registration or Order Form. Exceeding the licensed seat count requires prior written approval and adjustment of the licence fee.

### 1.4 Licence Term
This Agreement commences on the date of institutional account activation and continues for the term specified in the Order Form (typically 12 months), subject to renewal as described in Section 10.

## 2. Permitted Uses

The Platform may be used by Authorised Users solely for:

### 2.1 Academic Research
- Original scientific research in smart grid cybersecurity, ICS/SCADA security, critical infrastructure protection, and related disciplines;
- Development, training, and validation of machine learning and AI models for grid threat detection or resilience assessment;
- Formal research projects registered with the Institution's research management office;
- Collaborative research with other academic institutions, provided each collaborating institution holds a valid PYPY Grid licence.

### 2.2 Teaching and Education
- Classroom instruction, practical laboratory sessions, and tutorials in cybersecurity, power systems engineering, and related subjects;
- Development of course materials, case studies, and worked examples based on Platform simulation outputs;
- Student project supervision where the Platform is the designated simulation environment.

### 2.3 Publication
Authorised Users may publish research findings derived from Platform use, subject to the attribution requirements in Section 5.

---

## 3. Prohibited Commercial Use

### 3.1 Strict Prohibition on Commercial Activities
This licence **does not** grant any right to use the Platform for:
- Commercial consulting, professional penetration testing, or paid security assessments for third-party clients;
- Development of commercial products, tools, or services intended for resale;
- Technology transfer to for-profit entities without PYPY Grid's prior written consent;
- Contract research where the primary beneficiary is a for-profit commercial entity, unless a separate Commercial Research Addendum is executed.

### 3.2 Spin-out and Start-up Restriction
If Authorised Users intend to commercialise research conducted using the Platform (e.g., through a university spin-out or start-up), the Institution must notify PYPY Grid and execute a separate Commercial Licence or negotiate appropriate terms. PYPY Grid will not unreasonably withhold consent.

### 3.3 Training Data Restriction
Simulation output data generated on the Platform may not be incorporated into training datasets for commercial AI/ML products without express written consent from PYPY Grid.

---

## 4. Prohibition on Real-World Infrastructure Use

The Platform's simulation environments are strictly isolated from real-world infrastructure. The Institution and all Authorised Users must not use knowledge, tools, techniques, or data derived from the Platform to attack, probe, or interfere with any real-world power grid, ICS, or critical infrastructure. This prohibition is absolute and applies regardless of authorisation from the infrastructure owner, except where PYPY Grid has separately executed a Live Infrastructure Testing Agreement. See the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable_use_policy.md) for full details.

## 5. Attribution Requirements

### 5.1 Research Publications
When publishing academic papers, conference proceedings, technical reports, theses, or dissertations that incorporate simulation data, results, or methodologies derived from the Platform, Authorised Users **must** include the following acknowledgement:

> *"Simulations were conducted using the PYPY Grid Smart Grid Cybersecurity Research Platform (https://pypygrid.com), developed by PYPY Grid Sdn. Bhd., Malaysia."*

### 5.2 Recommended Citation Format
For the Platform itself (in lieu of a journal paper until a dedicated reference is published):

```
PYPY Grid Sdn. Bhd. (2026). PYPY Grid: Smart Grid Cybersecurity Research Platform
(Version 11.9). https://pypygrid.com
```

### 5.3 Presentations and Posters
Acknowledgement of Platform use should be included in oral presentations and research posters where results are derived from the Platform.

### 5.4 Media and Press
The Institution must not represent to press, media, or the public that PYPY Grid endorses the Institution's research findings, unless PYPY Grid has provided written confirmation of such endorsement.

## 6. Publication Rights

### 6.1 Right to Publish
PYPY Grid does not claim ownership over Authorised Users' research findings or publications. Authorised Users retain full publication rights over their work.

### 6.2 No Pre-Publication Review Required
PYPY Grid does not require pre-publication review of research based on Platform simulations, except in the rare circumstance where such research may reveal security vulnerabilities in the Platform itself, in which case responsible disclosure procedures (see Acceptable Use Policy, Section 4) apply before publication.

### 6.3 Open Access
PYPY Grid encourages Authorised Users to publish research outputs as open access where possible. PYPY Grid may, with the author's permission, feature published research on the PYPY Grid website and in promotional materials.

## 7. Data Handling

### 7.1 Research Data Ownership
All simulation data, experimental configurations, results, and derived datasets created by Authorised Users remain the intellectual property of the respective Authorised User and/or the Institution, subject to any applicable institutional IP policies.

### 7.2 Platform Data Processing
PYPY Grid processes personal data of Authorised Users as described in the [Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md). The Institution and PYPY Grid shall execute a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) for institutional accounts where required by GDPR or PDPA 2010.

### 7.3 No Upload of Real OT Data
Authorised Users must not upload real operational technology (OT) data, production SCADA logs, or live sensor data from real-world power infrastructure to the Platform without a specific written Data Handling Agreement executed with PYPY Grid.

### 7.4 Data Retention and Export
Simulation data is retained in accordance with the Platform's standard retention policy. The Institution may request bulk export of Authorised Users' data upon licence termination, subject to a 30-day processing window.

### 8.1 Access Management

### 8.2 Security
- Not share institutional account credentials with individuals outside the authorised user categories;
- Promptly report any suspected unauthorised use or security breach to security@pypygrid.com.

### 8.3 Compliance
- Ensure that Authorised Users comply with all applicable laws, including Malaysia's Computer Crimes Act 1997, applicable national cybersecurity laws, and any sector-specific regulations governing research with power system data.

### 8.4 Audit Cooperation
- Upon reasonable notice, cooperate with PYPY Grid in auditing compliance with seat limits and permitted use restrictions.
