# Acceptable Use Policy

**PYPY Grid — Smart Grid Cybersecurity Research Platform**
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2026
**Last Updated:** 1 July 2026

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") establishes the standards of conduct and use requirements for all users of the **PYPY Grid Smart Grid Cybersecurity Research Platform** ("Platform"), operated by **PYPY Grid Sdn. Bhd.**. This AUP applies to all users, including individuals, institutional users, researchers, students, and Enterprise licensees.

This AUP is incorporated into and forms part of the PYPY Grid [Terms of Service](terms_of_service.md). Violation of this AUP constitutes a violation of the Terms of Service and may result in immediate account suspension, termination, and referral to law enforcement.

PYPY Grid is a **research and simulation platform**. All attack scenarios, vulnerability analyses, and threat modelling exercises conducted on the Platform are executed in **isolated, virtualised simulation environments** that are entirely disconnected from real-world power grid infrastructure. This AUP exists to ensure that the Platform's capabilities are used exclusively for their intended research and educational purposes.

---

## 2. Authorised Use

PYPY Grid is designed and licensed exclusively for:

### 2.1 Academic and Scientific Research
- Cybersecurity research in the domain of smart grid, industrial control systems (ICS), and critical infrastructure protection;
- Publication of peer-reviewed research findings using simulation data generated on the Platform;
- PhD, master's, or undergraduate thesis work under supervision of accredited academic institutions.

### 2.2 Educational Activities
- Coursework, labs, and practical cybersecurity training within accredited educational programmes;
- Instructor-led demonstrations of attack scenarios, ICS protocols, and grid topology vulnerabilities;
- Student learning in power systems cybersecurity, SCADA security, and ICS/OT defence.

### 2.3 Professional Security Assessment (Authorised)
- Cybersecurity assessments of smart grid systems for which you hold explicit written authorisation from the asset owner;
- Red team/blue team exercises for utility companies, conducted by licensed professionals with documented scope agreements;
- National cybersecurity team exercises (e.g., NACSA Malaysia, CISA-affiliated bodies) with institutional registration.

### 2.4 Tool and Methodology Development
- Development and validation of detection algorithms, anomaly detection models, or intrusion prevention systems;
- Evaluation of grid protection standards (IEC 62351, NERC CIP) within simulation context;
- Testing and benchmarking of AI/ML models for grid threat detection.

---

## 3. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are **strictly prohibited** on the PYPY Grid Platform:

### 3.1 Real-World Infrastructure Attacks
You must **never** use knowledge, tools, configurations, scripts, or techniques derived from the PYPY Grid Platform to:
- Attack, probe, scan, disrupt, or gain unauthorised access to **any real-world power grid, SCADA system, PLC, RTU, or ICS device**;
- Interfere with the operation of any electricity generation, transmission, or distribution system;
- Conduct denial-of-service (DoS) or distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks on any operational infrastructure;
- Target communication networks used by utility providers, including SCADA communication channels, DNP3, Modbus, or IEC 61850 networks in production.

This prohibition applies globally and is absolute. There are **no exceptions** without a specific, documented authorisation from the asset owner **and** prior written approval from PYPY Grid.

### 3.2 Unauthorised System Access
- Accessing, exploiting, or attempting to exploit vulnerabilities in systems, networks, or devices for which you do not hold explicit written authorisation;
- Using the Platform to develop or test attack tools intended for use against third-party systems without authorisation.

### 3.3 Malicious Code and Exploits
- Uploading, sharing, or distributing functional malware, ransomware, worms, or exploits through the Platform;
- Embedding malicious payloads in shared Scenario Marketplace content;
- Attempting to compromise the Platform infrastructure itself.

### 3.4 Commercial Exploitation Without Authorisation
- Reselling, sublicensing, or commercially distributing Platform features, simulation results, or AI-generated content without PYPY Grid's prior written consent;
- Using the Platform for commercial penetration testing engagements without a valid Enterprise licence that explicitly authorises commercial use.

### 3.5 Misrepresentation and Fraud
- Falsely representing yourself as a researcher, academic, or authorised professional to obtain access to the Platform;
- Submitting false institutional affiliation information during registration or institutional onboarding.

### 3.6 Data Handling Violations
- Uploading real, production operational technology (OT) data, SCADA logs, or sensitive utility network data to the Platform without explicit approval from PYPY Grid and the data owner;
- Sharing other users' research data without their consent.

## 4. Responsible Disclosure

PYPY Grid is committed to maintaining the highest standards of platform security. If you discover a security vulnerability in the Platform itself, you are expected to act responsibly:

### 4.1 Responsible Disclosure Procedure
1. **Do not exploit** the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to confirm its existence.
2. **Report immediately** by emailing **security@pypygrid.com** with:
   - A clear description of the vulnerability;
   - Steps to reproduce;
   - Potential impact assessment;
   - Your contact information.
3. **Allow response time:** PYPY Grid will acknowledge your report within **48 hours** and provide an initial assessment within **7 business days**.
4. **Coordinate disclosure:** Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until PYPY Grid has had a reasonable opportunity to remediate it (typically **90 days** from confirmed receipt).

### 4.2 Safe Harbour
PYPY Grid will not take legal action against researchers who act in good faith under this responsible disclosure procedure, provided they do not access or modify user data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability.

### 4.3 Bug Bounty
PYPY Grid operates a discretionary recognition programme for responsibly disclosed vulnerabilities. Details at **https://pypygrid.com/security**.

## 5. Consequences of Violation

### 5.1 Enforcement Actions
PYPY Grid reserves the right to take any of the following actions, at its sole discretion and without prior notice, upon confirmed or suspected AUP violation:

- **Warning:** For minor or first-time technical violations;
- **Temporary suspension:** Account suspended for a defined period pending investigation;
- **Permanent termination:** Account permanently closed with no refund of prepaid fees;
- **Data preservation:** Preservation of account data and logs for legal proceedings;
- **Legal referral:** Reporting of the violation to:
  - Royal Malaysia Police (PDRM) Cybercrime Division;
  - Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC);
  - National Cyber Security Agency (NACSA);
  - Relevant law enforcement authorities in the user's jurisdiction;
  - Interpol Cybercrime Directorate where applicable.

### 5.2 Civil and Criminal Liability
Users who violate this AUP may face civil and criminal liability under:
- **Computer Crimes Act 1997 (Malaysia)** — sections 3, 4, 5 (unauthorised access and modification);
- **Communications and Multimedia Act 1998 (Malaysia)** — section 233 (improper use of network facilities);
- **EU Directive 2013/40/EU** — on attacks against information systems;
- **Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)** (if US-related conduct);
- Other applicable national cybercrime laws.

### 5.3 No Refund Upon Violation
Users whose accounts are terminated due to AUP violations are not entitled to any refund of subscription fees.
