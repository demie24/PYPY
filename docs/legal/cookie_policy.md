## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website or application. They enable the site to remember your actions and preferences over time, or between sessions. Cookies are not programmes and cannot carry viruses or install software on your device.

Similar technologies include:
- **Local Storage / Session Storage:** Browser-based storage used for maintaining session state within the PYPY Grid dashboard;
- **Pixel tags / Web beacons:** Tiny invisible images embedded in emails that allow us to track email open rates;
- **Fingerprinting:** We do **not** use browser fingerprinting.

## 5. Session Storage and Local Storage

In addition to cookies, the PYPY Grid dashboard application uses browser **localStorage** and **sessionStorage** to:
- Cache simulation state and experiment configuration during an active session;
- Store non-sensitive UI preferences;
- Improve dashboard responsiveness.

This data does not leave your browser and is not transmitted to our servers. It is cleared when you clear your browser data or log out.

## 6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

### 6.1 PYPY Grid Cookie Consent Manager
When you first visit the Platform, a Cookie Consent Banner will appear. You may:
- **Accept all cookies** (enables functional, analytics, and marketing categories);
- **Accept necessary only** (only strictly necessary cookies are set);
- **Customise preferences** (granular control per cookie category).

You may revisit and update your preferences at any time by clicking the **"Cookie Settings"** link in the Platform footer or at **https://pypygrid.com/cookie-settings**.

### 6.2 Browser-Level Controls
Most browsers allow you to control cookies through browser settings:
- **Google Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
- **Mozilla Firefox:** Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Microsoft Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Note that disabling all cookies may significantly impair your ability to use the Platform, including preventing login.

### 6.3 Opt-Out Links
- **Google Analytics:** https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
- **Google Ads:** https://adssettings.google.com/
- **Meta Pixel:** https://www.facebook.com/settings/?tab=ads
- **Mixpanel:** https://mixpanel.com/optout
- **Intercom:** Contact support@pypygrid.com to request chat session deletion.
